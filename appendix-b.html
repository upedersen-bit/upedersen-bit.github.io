21 Nov 1899 Herman, NE Dies; buried at Thone Cemetery
8 Dec 1899 Omaha Death notice in the Omaha World-Herald ("N. C. Olsen, aged 76")
1904–05 Denmark Sons Anders and Jens legally adopt the name Damborg
26 May 1910 Grant Twp. Census: his widow, Mary C. Olson, 80, living alone on her own income
1917 Washington Co. Probable death of his widow: "Mary N. Olson" in the Nebraska death index (not yet confirmed)
