Appendix A

Key facts

The facts in Appendices A and B are documented in the chapter notes.1
Born 1 June 1821, Hurup (Øster Hurup), Als Sogn, Hindsted Herred, Aalborg Amt
Baptised 2 June 1821 at home; presented in Als church 8 July 1821
Parents Ole Nielsen "Nordmand" (b. c. 1784, Norway) and Maren Jensdatter
Foster parents Christen Nielsen and Karen Nielsdatter, Skelund
Military Dragoon, 5th Dragoon Regiment, Randers, from 1 April 1844 to 1851; First Schleswig War
Occupation in Denmark Farm servant; lodger (indsidder) at Terndrup, 1855–66
Married (1) Kirsten Marie Jensen (1825–1874), 22 July 1854
Children Anders Christian (1854), Jens (1856), Dorthea Cathrine (1860), Ulrica Christine (1863)
Emigrated 1866, alone, with travel money from his wife's uncle Anders Stephansen; possibly landed at New York from The Queen on 2 May 1866, or at Quebec from the Moravian in June 1866; settled at New York Creek, Washington County, Nebraska
Married in Nebraska Anna Christiana Oleson, 59, on 23 February 1868, Washington County; then Mary Nelsen (b. c. 1830–36, Kongsberg, Norway), 20 February 1871, Blair, Nebraska; recorded as Caren (1880) and Mary C. (1885); a widow in Grant Township in 1910; probably died 1917, Washington County
Died 21 November 1899; buried Thone Cemetery, Herman, Washington County, Nebraska
Names used Niels Christian Olesen · N. C. Olesen Damborg · Niels Olsen · Nelse C. Olson
