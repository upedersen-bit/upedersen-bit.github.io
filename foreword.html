Foreword

This is the first part of a family history centred on Niels Christian Olesen.

At my baptism Damborg was entered as my middle name, as it was for my many cousins, and it was a puzzle to me what had led to this. Later, as an adult, Damborg took over, and the family name Pedersen was seldom used. Our children were likewise given the middle name Damborg, still without anyone really connecting anything in particular with the name. That is what aroused Ulrik's and my interest in exploring the origin of the name and finding out how it came into the family.

For me the search began with my paternal grandmother, Lovise, who, apparently without any real reason, had nine of her children give their children the middle name Damborg.

Further work in the church books showed that Lovise's mother, Ulrica, had carried the name on with the addition Damborg, though only at her burial. The search therefore led quite naturally on to her parents, among them her father, Niels Christian Olesen, who at Ulrica's baptism was entered as Niels Christian Olesen Damborg. That is rather interesting, because he does not use the name at the baptisms of his three other children, and his own parents apparently did not use it either.

This naturally turns the focus to Niels Christian Olesen himself. And it must be admitted that the course of his life is so fascinating that it widened the original aim of our search beyond simply answering the question of how the name Damborg came into the family.

Leo Pedersen
